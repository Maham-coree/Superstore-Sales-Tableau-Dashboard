# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to my Tableau project. 

In this project, I built an interactive dashboard using the classic **Sample Superstore dataset** to analyze how the business is performing in terms of sales, profits, and regional trends.

---

## 🎯 Why I Built This?
I wanted to create a simple, executive-ready dashboard that helps business managers quickly answer questions like:
* Which regions are bringing in the most revenue?
* Are high sales actually leading to profits?
* Which specific products are losing money?

## 💡 What I Found (Key Takeaways)
* **Overall Metrics:** The business generated over **$2.29M in Sales** with a net profit of **$286K+**.
* **Regional Performance:** The **West Region** is leading in total sales ($725K+), followed closely by the East.
* **Loss Makers Alert ⚠️:** High sales don't always mean profit! Products under **Tables** and **Bookcases** are actually running at a loss despite decent sales numbers.
* **Top Performers:** Technology and Office Supplies categories are the most reliable profit drivers.
